# Request to entertainment.ie for a listings feed

**To:** entertainment.ie, via the contact or advertising address on their site
**Subject:** Licensing your events listings for a household planning app

Hello,

I'm building Life Admin, an Irish app that helps households keep on top of bills and official post, and then plan their free time. Our "Free time" planner turns a free afternoon or evening into a short plan of things to do nearby. At the moment it uses open data from Fáilte Ireland and Sport Ireland.

Your gig, club and comedy listings would make it much more useful, and we'd like to do this properly rather than copy anything from your site. Would you consider licensing a feed (an API, RSS or CSV) of upcoming events?

What we would do with it:
- Show the event name, venue, date and time, and price.
- Link every listing back to entertainment.ie for details and tickets.
- Credit entertainment.ie clearly on every plan that uses your listings.
- Refresh once a day, and never resell or republish the data on its own.

We're happy to discuss terms, including a commercial arrangement or a referral link.
