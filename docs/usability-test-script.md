# Usability test script (Phase 0 and Phase 4)

Run the same script before and after the revamp, with 5 people per round (Jared Spool: watch real people; don't guess). Recruit from the target users: busy couples and families in Ireland or the UK who handle household bills, plus one person who manages admin for an elderly parent.

**Setup:** their own phone, the demo link, 20 minutes, think-aloud. Don't help unless they're stuck for over a minute. Record the screen if they agree.

## Warm-up (2 min)

- "How do you keep track of bills, renewals and official letters today?"
- "Tell me about the last time something like that caught you out."

## Tasks

Score each one: success / success with help / fail, plus time taken.

1. **Add a letter.** "Here's a letter (hand them a printed sample). Get it into the app."
2. **Find what's next.** "What's the next thing you need to deal with, and by when?" *(Target: under 5 seconds from opening the app; this is the experience vision.)*
3. **Fix a wrong date.** "The app read one date wrong. It should be the 20th. Fix it."
4. **Cancel a renewal.** "You don't want the car insurance to renew. Get an email ready to send."

## After the tasks

- "How do you feel right now, on a scale from 1 (more anxious) to 5 (calmer than before)?" *(Elizabeth Alli's measure: mindful design.)*
- "Did you trust the dates and amounts it found? What made you trust them, or not?" *(Ioana Teleanu: AI trust.)*
- "What would you change?"

## Report

For each round, record:
- task success rates;
- median times;
- the top 5 confusion points with quotes;
- the median calm score.

Compare round 1 with round 2. Each finding links to a metric: activation (3+ documents in week one), day-30 retention, deadlines caught, money saved, conversion.
